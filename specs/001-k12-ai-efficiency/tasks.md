# Implementation Tasks: AI's Impact on K-12 Classroom Efficiency

**Feature**: AI's Impact on K-12 Classroom Efficiency | **Status**: Planned
**Input**: Spec, Plan, Research, Data Model, Quickstart | **Output**: Research paper

## Summary

Research paper on AI's impact on K-12 classroom efficiency, specifically focusing on how AI applications can reduce teacher workload while improving student outcomes. The paper will target undergraduate economics students studying AI adoption in educational settings, with emphasis on return on investment analysis. The research will identify at least 3 concrete AI applications with supporting evidence, citing 8+ sources (80% peer-reviewed journals, 20% reputable news), with at least 75% of claims supported by empirical studies. The paper will follow APA 7th edition citation style, be 3,000-3,500 words in length, and be completed within 2 weeks.

## Dependencies

- Academic databases (ERIC, JSTOR, Google Scholar)
- Citation management tool (Zotero or Mendeley)
- Institutional library resources (if available)

## Parallel Execution Examples

- T001-T003 can run in parallel with T004-T005 during setup
- T008-P-T012-P can run in parallel during source identification
- T025-P-T028-P can run in parallel during deep dive phase

## Implementation Strategy

The paper will be developed incrementally across 4 phases:
1. Setup phase: Establish research environment and initial structure
2. Foundational phase: Identify and evaluate sources
3. User Story phases: Develop core paper sections in priority order
4. Polish phase: Finalize content, validate requirements, and prepare for submission

The MVP scope will be the completion of User Story 1: Research Paper Creation, which identifies 3 concrete AI applications with evidence and enables readers to explain ROI.
