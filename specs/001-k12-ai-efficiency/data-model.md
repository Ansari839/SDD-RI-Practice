# Data Model: AI's Impact on K-12 Classroom Efficiency

## Research Entities

### AI Application
- **Name**: The specific AI tool or system (e.g., automated grading, lesson planning assistant)
- **Category**: Type of application (grading, planning, assessment, etc.)
- **Description**: Functional overview of the AI application
- **Target Workflow**: Which teacher workflow is impacted (grading, lesson planning, assessment, etc.)
- **Implementation Cost**: Estimated cost for schools to deploy
- **Time Savings**: Quantified impact on teacher time (hours per week saved)
- **Evidence Level**: Strength of research evidence supporting effectiveness

### Teacher Workflow
- **Name**: The specific teaching activity (grading, lesson planning, parent communication, etc.)
- **Time Investment**: Average time spent on this activity per week
- **AI Enhancement Potential**: How AI can improve the workflow
- **Impact Category**: Effect on efficiency, quality, or both
- **Current Pain Points**: Common challenges teachers face with this workflow

### Student Outcome
- **Metric**: The measurable student outcome (test scores, engagement, learning pace, etc.)
- **Measurement Method**: How the outcome is assessed
- **AI Correlation**: Relationship between AI application and this outcome
- **Improvement Percentage**: Quantified improvement where available
- **Evidence Source**: Research study supporting the connection

### Educational Institution
- **Type**: Elementary, middle, or high school
- **Demographics**: Student population characteristics
- **Technology Infrastructure**: Current tech capabilities
- **Budget Constraints**: Financial limitations affecting AI adoption
- **Implementation Success**: Actual results from AI tool implementation

### Research Study
- **Title**: Name of the research paper
- **Authors**: List of researchers
- **Publication Year**: When the study was published
- **Methodology**: Research approach used
- **Sample Size**: Number of participants or schools studied
- **Key Findings**: Main results relevant to AI in education
- **Quality Score**: Assessment of research rigor

### ROI Component
- **Cost Element**: Various cost components (software, training, infrastructure, etc.)
- **Benefit Type**: Different types of benefits (time savings, improved outcomes, etc.)
- **Quantification Method**: How the cost or benefit is measured
- **Value**: The estimated financial or time value
- **Validation Source**: Where the value comes from (study, vendor, etc.)

## Relationships

### AI Application → Teacher Workflow
- **Relationship**: "enhances"
- **Impact**: Reduction in time spent or improvement in quality
- **Evidence**: Research studies supporting the impact

### AI Application → Student Outcome
- **Relationship**: "affects"
- **Correlation**: How the AI application influences student outcomes
- **Evidence**: Studies showing the connection

### Teacher Workflow → Student Outcome
- **Relationship**: "influences"
- **Connection**: How changes to teacher workflows affect student outcomes
- **Evidence**: Research linking workflow efficiency to student success

### Educational Institution → AI Application
- **Relationship**: "implements"
- **Result**: Success/failure of implementation and outcomes
- **Factors**: Conditions affecting success

### Research Study → AI Application
- **Relationship**: "evaluates"
- **Focus**: Which applications were tested in the study
- **Findings**: Results specifically related to the application

### ROI Component → AI Application
- **Relationship**: "applies_to"
- **Relevance**: How the cost or benefit component relates to the AI application

## Validation Rules

1. Each AI Application must be supported by at least one peer-reviewed research study
2. Each Student Outcome must be measurable and quantifiable where possible
3. All evidence cited must be published within the last 10 years
4. All cost and benefit estimates must include their source
5. Each Teacher Workflow must have documented time investment metrics
6. ROI calculations must be based on empirical data, not projections
7. Educational Institution characteristics must reflect real data, not hypotheticals

## State Transitions (for implementation studies)

- **Planning Phase**: Institution considering AI implementation
- **Implementation Phase**: AI tool being deployed
- **Early Usage Phase**: Initial usage with learning curve
- **Stabilized Usage Phase**: Full adoption achieved
- **Evaluation Phase**: Impact assessment and measurement
- **Optimization Phase**: Improvements and refinements made
