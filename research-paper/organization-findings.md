## T029: Calculate Potential ROI Metrics from Data in Sources

### ROI Calculation Methodology
ROI = (Benefits - Investment) / Investment × 100

For our calculations:
- Benefits = Time value savings + (optional) Student outcome improvements
- Investment = Cost of AI tool per teacher per year
- Time value = $40/hour (estimated teacher time value)

### Detailed ROI Calculations

#### 1. AI-Powered Grading Systems
- Time saved: 5-7 hours/week × 35 weeks = 175-245 hours/year
- Time value: $7,000-$9,800/year per teacher
- Tool cost: $75-150/year per teacher
- ROI: (7000-75)/75 to (9800-150)/150 = 9,233% to 6,433%
- (These are very high ROIs because time value is high compared to tool costs)

#### 2. AI-Driven Lesson Planning Assistants
- Time saved: 4-6 hours/week × 35 weeks = 140-210 hours/year
- Time value: $5,600-$8,400/year per teacher
- Tool cost: $62.50-125/year per teacher (averaging the range)
- ROI: (5600-62.5)/62.5 to (8400-125)/125 = 8,860% to 6,620%

#### 3. AI-Powered Assessment and Diagnostic Tools
- Time saved: 3-4 hours/week × 35 weeks = 105-140 hours/year
- Time value: $4,200-$5,600/year per teacher
- Tool cost: $100-175/year per teacher (averaging the range)
- ROI: (4200-100)/100 to (5600-175)/175 = 4,100% to 3,100%

### Adjusted ROI with Conservative Time Valuation ($20/hour)
- Grading tools: 175-245 hours × $20 = $3,500-$4,900; ROI: 2,233% to 3,167%
- Lesson planning: 140-210 hours × $20 = $2,800-$4,200; ROI: 2,133% to 3,229%
- Assessment tools: 105-140 hours × $20 = $2,100-$2,800; ROI: 1,114% to 1,500%
