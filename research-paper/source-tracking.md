## Column Definitions

- **ID**: Unique identifier for the source
- **Title**: Full title of the publication
- **Authors**: List of all authors
- **Year**: Publication year
- **Type**: Article type (empirical study, literature review, theoretical, etc.)
- **Focus Area**: Primary focus (grading, lesson planning, assessment, etc.)
- **Evidence Level**: Strength of evidence (high, medium, low)
- **Time Savings**: Documented time savings for teachers
- **Outcome Improvements**: Documented improvements in student outcomes
- **Cost/Benefit**: Cost-benefit analysis results if available
- **Source Quality Score**: Score based on methodology and credibility (1-10)
- **Notes**: Additional notes and observations

## Key Metrics to Track

### Research Quality Metrics
- Percentage of empirical vs. theoretical studies
- Average source quality score
- Publication year distribution
- Peer-review status

### Content Metrics
- Number of sources per AI application type
- Number of sources with ROI data
- Number of sources with time savings data
- Number of sources with student outcome data

### Target Metrics
- Minimum 8 sources for final paper
- At least 75% empirical studies
- Sources published within last 10 years
- At least 3 sources per AI application area
