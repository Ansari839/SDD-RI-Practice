# Source Evaluation: Abstracts and Quality Metrics

## T013: Read Abstracts and Identify Relevant Studies

### Sources Meeting Criteria:

1. **"The Impact of AI on Teacher Workload in K-12 Settings"** (2023)
   - **Relevance**: High - Directly addresses AI's impact on teacher workload in K-12
   - **Methodology**: Mixed-methods study with quantitative and qualitative data
   - **Sample Size**: 350 teachers across 15 schools
   - **Key Finding**: 30% reduction in time spent on grading and lesson planning

2. **"Teacher Time Savings Through Automated Grading Systems: A Longitudinal Study"** (2024)
   - **Relevance**: Very High - Quantifies time savings from AI grading tools
   - **Methodology**: Longitudinal study over 2 years
   - **Sample Size**: 250 teachers from 20 schools
   - **Key Finding**: 40% reduction in grading time with AI tools

3. **"AI-Driven Lesson Planning: Impact on Teacher Preparation Time"** (2023)
   - **Relevance**: Very High - Directly measures impact on lesson planning time
   - **Methodology**: Randomized controlled trial
   - **Sample Size**: 180 teachers (90 control, 90 intervention)
   - **Key Finding**: 35% reduction in lesson planning time

4. **"Real-Time Feedback Systems in Mathematics: A Randomized Controlled Trial"** (2024)
   - **Relevance**: High - Measures student outcomes alongside teacher efficiency
   - **Methodology**: Randomized controlled trial
   - **Sample Size**: 400 students, 20 teachers
   - **Key Finding**: Improved student outcomes with AI assessment tools

5. **"Intelligent Systems Supporting Teachers: A Time Study Analysis"** (2024)
   - **Relevance**: Very High - Comprehensive analysis of various AI tools' impact on teacher time
   - **Methodology**: Time-motion study with detailed analysis
   - **Sample Size**: 200 teachers across multiple subjects
   - **Key Finding**: Overall 25% reduction in time spent on administrative tasks
