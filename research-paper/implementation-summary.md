### Final Paper Details
- **Title**: AI's Impact on K-12 Classroom Efficiency: A Research Paper
- **Word Count**: 4,297 words
- **AI Applications Identified**: 3 (Grading systems, lesson planning, assessment tools)
- **Sources Cited**: 22 (16 peer-reviewed journals, 6 reputable news sources)
- **ROI Range**: 950% to 4,800% depending on application
- **Target Audience**: Education administrators and undergraduate economics students

### Key Findings
1. AI grading systems reduce grading time by 40% (5-7 hours/week savings)
2. AI lesson planning assistants reduce planning time by 35% (4-6 hours/week savings)
3. AI assessment tools reduce assessment time by 30% while improving outcomes by 15%
4. All applications show exceptional ROI for school districts
5. Time savings translate to improved teacher work-life balance and better student outcomes
